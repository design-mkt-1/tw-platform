# Visual fidelity sweep — 2026-09-12

Seven workers measured the running build at 390x844 against the numbers already committed in
`docs/design-inventory/`. No Figma MCP calls were spent: the sweep reads the recorded measurements
and compares them with what the browser renders.

Each worker owned one inventory area and was told the rest was off limits. They returned raw
numbers — selector, expected, measured, delta — and were forbidden to write "correct" or "looks
right", because the failure this split exists to prevent already happened once on this project: an
early worker reported `#080814` as "the app header background" after measuring nodes `1:6594` and
`1:6617`, two dead JACKPOT headers pasted on the canvas.

## What was wrong, and what was fixed

### 1. The whole casino page was spaced 8px too far apart at every section boundary

**The largest single finding of the sweep.** `src/app/page.tsx` set `gap-6` (24px) between sections.
The design's own vertical table (`04-casino-rows-c.md:302-318`) gives every grid wrapper as 370 tall
and every tournament wrapper as 276, and `03-casino-rows-b.md:138` lists `16` as the section gap and
the section top pad in the same breath. The content inside those wrappers measures 354 and 260,
which is exactly what this build renders — so 354 + 16 = 370 reproduces the pitch and 354 + 24 does
not.

Measured before: the page was **5734** tall against the frame's **5628.7**, and `Популярне` began at
y 486 where the design begins it at 430. After the fix `Популярне` begins at **430**, `Нові Ігри` at
**800** and `Провідні провайдери` at **1170** — the design's numbers to the pixel.

The first three bands are different and the same table says so: the hero runs 60..292, the category
switcher 292..336 and the ticker 336..414, each starting exactly where the last one ends, with no
gap at all. They are now one flex child of their own and the 16px pitch begins below them.

### 2. The bottom navigation plate lost a pixel off its right edge

`BottomNavBar.tsx` asks for a 391x69 plate offset by `-0.5px`, because the export carries half a
pixel of stroke bleed on every side of the 390x68 artwork. Tailwind's preflight sets
`img { max-width: 100% }`, so inside a 390px shell that plate resolved to a computed width of
**exactly 390px** — measured, not inferred. The left offset still took its half pixel and the right
edge lost one, so the compensation only ever worked on one side.

`BonusCarousel.tsx:66` already carried `max-w-none` for the same reason. This is the class of bug
this project keeps producing: fixed where it was noticed, missed where it was not. A sweep of every
`<img>` on `/`, `/sport`, `?panel=menu` and `?panel=search` found the nav plate to be the only
remaining one.

### 3. The menu panel's own right border was narrowing everything behind it

The two contact buttons measured **168.5** and **166.5** where the design draws both at 168 — two
buttons a player sees side by side, 2px apart.

Two causes stacked, and the first guess was wrong. `min-w-0` was tried first, on the theory that a
flex item's default `min-width: auto` was refusing to shrink; it changed nothing, which is recorded
here because it was measured rather than argued. The real pair:

- `flex-1` is `flex: 1 1 0%`, and a `flex-basis` of 0 sizes the **content** box. Support carries a
  1px outline and Vip Manager does not, so the border was added on top of an otherwise equal share:
  free space 333, split 166.5 each, plus 2px on one of them.
- The panel's `border-r` — the design's `border-right: 1px solid #D8DDE7` on `1:6817` — is inside
  the box, so a panel exactly as wide as the viewport had 389px of content. Every row inside laid
  out in 357 instead of 358, and the pair could no longer fit `168 + 8 + 168 = 344`.

The border is now a pseudo-element, which draws the same line without taking layout width, and both
buttons carry the drawn 168. Measured after: panel **390**, buttons **168 / 168**, no horizontal
scroll in any of the three variants.

## What was measured and deliberately not changed

Each of these looked like a defect in a worker's table and turned out to be a decision already
written down. They are listed so the next sweep does not re-open them.

- **The search panel ships one provider carousel row where the design stacks two.** Cost: the empty
  panel measures 254 against the drawn 336, and the suggestions panel 351 against 496.
  `SearchOverlay.tsx` states the reason — the second row is a byte-identical duplicate of the first,
  the same five providers in the same order, so it falls under the repeated-placeholder rule.
  **This is the one deviation worth putting back in front of the owner**, because it is the largest
  remaining gap between a built panel and its frame.
- **The carousel indicator renders in all four search states** where `09-search-states.md:198` files
  it under "State not found only". `SearchOverlay.tsx:442-444` argues it belongs to the carousel
  rather than to the empty state, and only `no-results` carries the carousel's *label*, which is
  what the inventory actually measured.
- **The menu panel renders 776 tall in all three variants** against the drawn 742 / 656.
  `MenuPanel.tsx:113` chose `min-h-full` on purpose, and `08-menus.md:290-295` shows the design
  filling the same band with the same `#F4F6FA`.
- **`Реєстрація`, the varied league fixtures, the `310 / 84 / 46` filter counts** — all three are the
  owner's recorded decisions, and all three were re-reported by workers who had not been told.
- **`Політика конфіденційності` wraps to two lines inside `w-[141px]`** — the design draws it on two
  lines (`05-casino-footer.md:242`). Measured this session: `scrollWidth` and `clientWidth` both 141,
  two client rects, row height still 44.
- **The partner logos are WebP, not the PNGs `manifest-footer.md` records.** There are zero `.png`
  files anywhere under `public/images/`; the manifest's "conversion not run" note is stale.

## Still open, and why nothing was guessed

### Measured defects not yet fixed

**The provider row `1:3762` renders 136 tall against the drawn 248.** Three parts, and only two are
unambiguous:

1. Its header measures 24 where the design gives 40, and its gap 8 where the design gives 4.
2. The design draws a **40x40 round search button** in that header (`02-casino-rows-a.md:19`,
   `:153-160`, `:253`) and the build renders no third child at all. The asset is already harvested —
   `public/images/icons/search-header.svg`, exported as `SEARCH_ICON`.
3. The design draws **two** horizontal provider tracks and the build renders one. This one is not
   safe to act on: `02-casino-rows-a.md` UNKNOWN #6 flags the two-track reading as unresolved in the
   design itself.

That deficit is why `Рекомендовані` still begins at 1322 against the design's 1434, and why the page
now measures 5582 against 5628.7 — under by 46.7 where it was over by 105.3.

**Parts 1 and 2 fixed on 2026-09-10 (the session after this sweep).** `SectionHeader` already had
the slot and the comment naming this caller; `page.tsx` had never filled it. It now renders the
header's own `SearchButton` — the same component as the two app-header search buttons, since
`docs/tokens.md` lists `1:3781` under the same `--surface-muted` token — with `className="gap-1"`.
The design draws the button but not what it opens; it opens the search sheet, whose title is the
same string as this row, `Провідні провайдери`.

One thing the first attempt got wrong and the measurement caught: with `gap-2` as a base class and
the caller's `gap-1` appended, the header still read `gap: 8px`. Tailwind emits `.gap-1` before
`.gap-2`, so the base won. `gap-2` is now the prop's default rather than a base class.

### Questions for the owner

1. **The search panel's second carousel row** — put it back for fidelity, or keep the one row under
   the repeated-placeholder rule? It is 96px of panel height in two of the four search states.
2. **`Лайв казіно` or `Лайф казіно`?** The bottom tab ships `Лайв`; the design writes `Лайф казіно`
   (`10-navbar-headers.md:92`) and `Лайф казино` (`08-menus.md:307`). The design is inconsistent with
   itself — the category chip is `лайв Казіно` — and the build normalised to the chip's spelling.
   Nothing records that decision. `Лайф` is "life"; `Лайв` is "live".
3. **The currency format.** The tournament prize renders `2 000 ₴` where the design draws a bare
   `2000`, and the balance `140,00 ₴` where the design draws `$ 140.00`. `formatUahWhole` is the only
   place in `src` that touches `Intl`, deliberately. The design is already inconsistent across
   adjacent screens: `₴250.000` on the hero, `$ 140.00` on the balance chip, `41.04 GBP` in the
   ticker.
4. **`Купон` has no `onClick`**, because the Figma file draws no bet-slip panel for it to open.
   Either it joins the register of controls the design gives no target, or the panel needs designing.
5. **The search suggestion row's copy.** It renders `Pragmatic` and a computed `20 ігор`; the design
   writes the literals `Pragmatic Play` and `245 ігор`. The name comes from the provider catalogue
   and the count from the games in it.

## The tooling that came with the sweep

Three of the four category chips had no address, so `review.mjs` and `a11y.mjs` had never rendered
them. `?category=slots | live-casino | crash` now reaches them, `popular` is omitted exactly as
`postlogin` is, and the registry is 14 rows across 11 frames rather than 11 rows.

**The `expectText` for those rows was measured, not reasoned**, and the measurement contradicted the
obvious choice. Loading all four states and diffing `document.body.innerText`:

| state | line unique to it |
| --- | --- |
| `slots` | `Wild Cash x9990` |
| `live-casino` | `Mini Roulette` |
| `crash` | none |
| `popular` | none |

`popular` renders **neither** `Wild Cash x9990` nor `Mini Roulette` — the `limit: 6` cut drops them —
so pinning `crash` with those two as rejects would have been vacuous against the plain `/`. It is
pinned instead with `Sweet Bonanza` (drawn by popular and slots) and `Mega Roulette` (drawn by
popular and live-casino), plus `Aviator` as an expect, because a reject-only row also passes on a
blank page.

`Screen` now declares `expectText` and `rejectText`, which the JSON had carried since 2026-09-11
surviving only on the `as Screen[]` cast — a misspelled `expectTest` would have been silently
ignored and the row would have read as guarded. `AUTH_MODES`, `PANELS` and `CATEGORY_IDS` are
exported from `UrlStateBridge.tsx` and imported by the test, which had restated them and said in its
own comment that it wanted this.
